# Articles

### Tutorials

- [Estimate
  PSFs](https://tomwenseleers.github.io/logconNMF/articles/estimate-psf.md):

  Known convolution, blind kernel estimation and calibrated constant or
  time-varying point spread functions.

- [Sparse log-concave NMF with automatic best rank
  tuning](https://tomwenseleers.github.io/logconNMF/articles/log-concave-nmf.md):

  Recover nonnegative profiles and loadings with flexible noise models,
  hard shape constraints and automatic rank selection.

- [Simultaneous multisample log-concave NMF with automatic best rank
  tuning](https://tomwenseleers.github.io/logconNMF/articles/simultaneous-nmf.md):

  Share spectra across aligned samples with log-concave profiles,
  missing compounds and flexible noise models.

### Algorithm and theory

- [Algorithms and theory for PSFs and sparse log-concave
  factorization](https://tomwenseleers.github.io/logconNMF/articles/algorithm.md):

  Likelihoods, initialization, shape constraints, rank objectives and
  mathematical pseudocode for single- and multisample models.
